Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. Introduction

Cutcore Upholstery ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our day-to-day business dealings, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

2. Information Officer

For the purposes of POPIA, enquiries regarding this policy or your personal information can be directed to our Information Officer at info@cutcoreupholstery.co.za or 082 594 8002.

3. What information we collect

CategoryExamples
Contact detailsName, phone number, email address, physical or delivery address
Enquiry & job detailsPhotos of furniture, descriptions of work requested, fabric or material preferences
Payment informationProof of payment or EFT references (we do not store full card details)
Technical dataIP address, browser type and pages visited, collected via cookies and analytics when you use this website
CommunicationsRecords of calls, WhatsApp messages, and emails relating to your enquiry or order

4. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you — through our contact form, WhatsApp, phone calls, email, or in person at the workshop — and automatically through cookies and similar technologies when you browse this website (see Section 8).

5. Why we use your information

  • To provide quotes and carry out upholstery work you request
  • To contact you about the progress, collection or delivery of your order
  • To process payments and keep the financial records we are legally required to keep
  • To respond to general enquiries and provide customer support
  • To improve this website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with legal, tax and regulatory obligations

We process your information on the basis that it is necessary to perform a contract with you, to comply with a legal obligation, or with your consent (for example, when you opt in to marketing communications).

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Fabric, foam and material suppliers, where necessary to fulfil your order
  • Payment processors or our bank, to process payments
  • IT, hosting, email, or analytics service providers who help us run this website and business
  • Professional advisors (accountants, legal counsel) where required
  • Regulators or authorities where required by law

Any third party we share information with is required to protect it in line with POPIA and to use it only for the purpose we specify.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements (typically up to 5 years for financial records, in line with South African tax law). Photos of completed work may be retained for our portfolio only with your separate consent.

8. Cookies

This website uses cookies to remember your preferences and to understand site usage through analytics. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit, and you can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Essential cookies required for the site to function are not affected by this choice.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information appropriately protected.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you and access it
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate, irrelevant or outdated information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details in Section 2. The Information Regulator can be reached at inforegulator.org.za.

11. Children's information

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a parent or guardian as required by POPIA.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The version published on this page is the one currently in effect.

13. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@cutcoreupholstery.co.za or 082 594 8002, or in person at 282 Samantha St, Strydompark, Randburg, Gauteng 2194.
