Service area questions

Is there an upholsterer near me?

Cutcore Upholstery is based at 282 Samantha St, Strydompark, Randburg, and collects and delivers across greater Johannesburg, including Sandton, Fourways, Rosebank, Melville, Roodepoort, Krugersdorp and Centurion.

Do you collect and deliver furniture?

Yes. We work within roughly a 50km radius of Randburg. Tell us your suburb when you request a quote and we will confirm collection and delivery arrangements in writing.

My suburb is not listed. Can you still help?

Possibly. Send us your suburb and a few photos on WhatsApp on 082 594 8002 and we will tell you whether we can collect your furniture.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp a few photos of the piece, or use the contact form. We give a written quote covering fabric, foam, frame repairs and timeline before any work or deposit.
