About us

A small workshop that takes furniture seriously

Cutcore Upholstery is an independent upholstery workshop based in Strydompark, Randburg. We work on one piece at a time, using traditional hand techniques alongside modern materials.

Our approach

Repair what can be repaired, rebuild what can't

Reupholstery only makes sense when the frame underneath is worth keeping. Before we quote, we check joints, webbing and springs so you know whether a recover, a partial rebuild, or a full strip-down is the right call — and why.

We supply fabric and foam from trusted local suppliers, or work with material you already own. Either way, every quote is written down before work begins, with no surprise add-ons at collection.

What guides us

The principles behind every job

Honest quoting

We tell you when a repair isn't worth it — and we say so before you pay a deposit, not after.

Hand-finished work

Stitching, piping and tacking are still done by hand where it matters most for durability and finish.

Built to last

We'd rather reinforce a frame properly once than patch it twice — workmanship is backed by warranty.
